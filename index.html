Auckland road crash analytics
Power BI · SQL · Python · Microsoft Fabric
If the preview doesn't load, open the PDF directly ↗

Business problem

Auckland's road crash data was spread across raw incident records, making it hard for planners to see where and why crashes cluster, or which factors (road type, time of day, weather) drive severity.

What it solves

A predictive analytics model built on Microsoft Fabric surfaces hotspot locations and severity patterns in an interactive Power BI report, turning scattered records into a clear, explorable risk map.

Recommendations

  • Prioritise engineering review (signage, lighting, speed limits) at the top-ranked hotspot intersections first.
  • Target enforcement and public-awareness campaigns around the time-of-day and weather conditions most linked to severe crashes.
  • Re-run the model quarterly as new crash data arrives, so the hotspot ranking stays current rather than static.

One NZ sentiment analysis
Python · VADER / RoBERTa · Power BI
If the preview doesn't load, open the PDF directly ↗

Business problem

One NZ's product team had a large volume of customer reviews and app-store ratings but no fast, reliable way to track sentiment shifts or spot emerging complaint themes.

What it solves

NLP sentiment scoring applied to review text, visualised in Power BI as a sentiment trend line alongside complaint category and churn-risk indicators.

Recommendations

  • Route the top negative-sentiment complaint categories directly to the relevant product team each month.
  • Track sentiment before/after major app releases to catch regressions early.
  • Set a sentiment-score threshold that automatically flags a review cluster for manual review.

Smartwatch health analytics
Python · clustering · Power BI
If the preview doesn't load, open the PDF directly ↗

Business problem

Clinicians monitoring wearable health data had no fast way to identify which patients were most at risk of a fall or decline in activity, among large volumes of sensor readings.

What it solves

Clustering and predictive models flag fall-risk probability, sleep quality and activity levels, surfaced in a Power BI dashboard clinicians can scan at a glance.

Recommendations

  • Prioritise clinical follow-up for patients flagged in the highest fall-risk band each week.
  • Cross-reference sleep-quality decline with fall-risk score to catch early warning combinations.
  • Extend the model with additional sensor inputs (heart rate variability) to improve early detection.

About me

I spent 12 years verifying and building safety‑critical avionics software for Boeing, Honeywell, Pratt & Whitney and Bombardier - environments where precision, compliance and evidence‑based decisions were non‑negotiable. Working on systems where a single missed requirement could ground an aircraft taught me discipline, structured thinking and a deep respect for quality.

After a long and rewarding engineering career, I took a short break to support my son during an important developmental phase. That experience strengthened my resilience, perspective and ability to balance complex responsibilities, qualities I now bring back into my professional life.

Returning to work, I chose to upskill and completed a Master of Business Informatics, driven by my interest in modern technologies and the rapid evolution of AI across industries. Today, I’m focused on roles that blend engineering rigour with analytics, automation and emerging digital tools to create meaningful impact.

Outside work, I enjoy challenging myself, staying curious and making each day count. I believe in continuous learning, humility and not taking personal or professional growth for granted.
